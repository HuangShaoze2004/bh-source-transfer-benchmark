# Journal scope and subscription route audit

Checked 2026-11-10 UTC against publisher pages. This is a scope check for an
experimental evidence package, not a submission plan or an acceptance forecast.

| Journal | Official scope and fit | Subscription / non-OA route |
|---|---|---|
| Reaction Chemistry & Engineering | [Official journal page](https://www.rsc.org/publishing/journals/reaction-chemistry-and-engineering) includes reaction development, optimization, screening, and digitalisation of chemistry through machine learning and algorithms. A computational feasibility study could fit if it establishes a useful, reproducible reaction-science result under realistic transfer limits. Mere application of established modules is not evidence of editorial novelty. | Official page identifies the journal as hybrid, offers subscription or gold OA, and states that ordinary submission/publication has no fees. Gold OA is an optional paid route. The present project authorizes no payment or submission. |
| Chemical Papers | [Official aims and scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/11696/aims-and-scope) covers basic/applied chemistry, chemical engineering and interdisciplinary studies. The chemical relevance of the feasibility task and the quality of controls would need to be clear. | [Official publishing options](https://link.springer.com/journal/11696/how-to-publish-with-us) explicitly offers the subscription publishing model with no APC, as well as optional OA. Publishing model selection follows acceptance. Institutional/funder OA obligations can affect suitability of a subscription route. |

These journal policies can change. Recheck publisher pages if an author later
decides to prepare a submission. No journal is promised to accept computational
results, a small improvement, or a negative result. READY_FOR_AUTHOR_REVIEW, if
eventually reached, means that the project's strong controls and confirmatory
evaluation are complete with a credible increment; it does not imply journal
acceptance. A complete negative study remains a valid deliverable with NOT_READY
and a documented explanation.

Only bibliographic/policy metadata and original summaries are stored here.
Publisher websites' general text-reuse rights were not assumed from public
access. No manuscript, cover letter, submission account, or external message
was created during this audit.
